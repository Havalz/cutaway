# Credits

## Camera hero replica

This work is based on "Canon EOS 5D Mark IV" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/canon-eos-5d-mark-iv-33001ed6f05042d89ac8690ffaaed0a4) by RAW (https://sketchfab.com/ocbsketch) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes made for Cutaway: lens split from the body, textures resized to 1024 px, material replaced with an emissive ghost shell, and code-drawn internal parts (mirror, prism, shutter, sensor, lens elements) added. The internal parts are not from the original model.

Include this credit wherever the Lens or its footage is shared (Devpost page, video description).
